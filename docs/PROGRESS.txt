OPERATIONAL LIMITS
The first successful log directory stays bound for the module lifetime. Fixed
log filenames are reused; preserve evidence before another session. The module
pin lasts until process exit, including after clean stop. Retained keeps
resources/code alive and rejects another start; never force unloading.
Remote-thread timeouts leave scratch memory because the thread may still use
it. Load/start/status/stop were recorded successfully in one 3576 session;
this does not establish lifecycle behavior across loads or gameplay compatibility.
The historical PID 39068 capture stored 16-byte prefixes and 64 records. The
experimental collector stores up to 4096 bytes and 256 records. Neither archive
is replay input. Final stop counters are authoritative.
