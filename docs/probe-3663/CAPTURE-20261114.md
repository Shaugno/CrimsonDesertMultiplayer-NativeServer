## Hypotheses

The three roughly six-second stage gaps are the live cost of the initial
identity check and the two admission checks. That fits where the stage lines
are written, and it is larger than the file-backed scan of the same image.
This run does not split `VirtualQuery` out of those intervals, and it does
not explain the 2026-11-13 timeout. A start that takes the same path can
still cross 20 seconds. The wait was not raised.

The stored histograms differ between the labeled windows. That is compatible
with the action changing the pre-ready receive mix. It is also compatible
with unrelated receive traffic during the first 1.2 to 3.1 seconds after the
receive hook. The files do not separate those explanations. They cannot
identify a movement field, because no stored row is in the accepted
post-ready window and no send row was stored.
